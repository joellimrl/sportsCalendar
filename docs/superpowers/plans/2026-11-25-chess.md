# Chess calendar implementation plan

**Goal:** Show accurate published dates for the open classical FIDE World Championship and Candidates, without news.

**Architecture:** Discover editions and official event sites through FIDE's public WordPress API using a current-year through current-year-plus-two window. Read published event schedules, preferring explicit UTC timestamps. Preserve last-known-good events independently by edition and round. No season dates, player lists, or deployment-specific Salesforce identifiers in production code.

**Tech stack:** Existing dependency-free Node.js generator and vanilla browser UI.

**Scope and decisions:** Reuse tournament bars and individual round chips, add a Chess toggle, convert published times to SGT, and label conditional tiebreaks. Omit times when the source timezone is unknown. Undated announcements do not create calendar events. Official event schedules outrank announcement prose. No news feed or live results. The user approved proceeding with dates as the priority.

## Review focus

- Bidding windows, qualification dates, and women's/rapid/youth competitions must not become open classical event periods.
- Explicit timezone offsets and SGT day rollover must survive conversion; unknown offsets must not be guessed.
- A partial or failed edition must not delete the other edition or cached rounds.
- Changed schedules must replace the same round ID, without duplicates.
- Discovery must continue into future editions without hardcoded fixture dates or Salesforce deployment hashes.
